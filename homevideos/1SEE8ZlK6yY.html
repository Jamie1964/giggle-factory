Revisit the most viral laughter-inducing moments of the past year! Funniest Fails of the Year Review delivers a high-energy round-up showcasing the absolute best home videos submitted throughout the season.

From backyard birthday piñata swings going wildly off-target to summer pool jump miscalculations and pets taking over holiday gatherings, this compilation brings together the most memorable, feel-good family bloopers under one roof.
