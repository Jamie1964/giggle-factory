Complete Video Description

"Best Security Camera Fails" highlights the most hilarious moments captured when people forgot the cameras were rolling. Real doorbell camera footage capturing pets taking over, awkward deliveries, and neighborhood slips on ice.

Where, What, & How It Happened

Recorded on smart video doorbells and home security systems, these clips show authentic everyday mishaps on front porches and front yards. Because these static cameras record automatically upon detecting motion, they capture candid comedy that would normally go unnoticed.
