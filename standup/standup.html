Comedy Fun Facts

  • George Carlin performed over 14 HBO specials.
  • Gabriel Iglesias owns 800+ Volkswagen buses.
  • Kevin Hart’s “Laugh at My Pain” sold out in 90 cities.
  • Robin Williams improvised so much directors used multiple cameras.
  • Dave Chappelle walked away from $50 million.
  • Jo Koy started at a Las Vegas coffee shop open mic.
  • Jim Carrey performed his first stand‑up set at age 15 — and bombed so hard he didn’t return for two years.
  • Kevin Hart’s first comedy club paycheck was $45 — and he spent $40 of it on gas to get home.
  • Jo Koy once sold out an arena in Hawaii faster than Bruno Mars.
  • Bill Burr recorded his famous “Philadelphia Rant” after being booed for 10 minutes straight — and won the crowd back.
  • Robin Williams improvised so much during “Aladdin” that the script doubled in size.
  • Dave Chappelle once walked onstage in Ohio and performed for over 4 hours with no material prepared.
  • Ali Wong wrote most of “Baby Cobra” while actually pregnant — and filmed it at 7½ months.
  • George Lopez was the first Latino comedian to headline a sold‑out show at the Staples Center.
  • John Mulaney originally wanted to be a writer only — he didn’t plan to perform stand‑up at all.
  • Ralphie May lost a comedy competition to Sam Kinison — who then mentored him for years.
  • Hasan Minhaj’s “Homecoming King” was rejected by multiple networks before Netflix picked it up.
  • Fluffy (Gabriel Iglesias) owns so many Hawaiian shirts he has a warehouse section dedicated just to them.
